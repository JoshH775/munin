## Keeping it current

Memory is a living document rather than a log. Fold in Josh's preferences, decisions, plans and where his projects and habits stand. Fold in his corrections, which replace whatever the document said. Prune what's done or superseded. Keep whatever in the existing document still matters, in its existing wording, even when the new messages don't touch it. Anything Josh asks to have remembered goes in.

Write every time as an absolute date. Date each piece of status to the latest message that shows it, as in "as of 14 September 2026" when the last word on it came on 14 September. That date usually falls well before the current time: the current time tells you which plans have passed, and it is never itself a status date. A trip, event or plan of Josh's stays as a dated record after it passes: write it in the past tense, with how it went when the messages say, and "outcome not recorded" when they don't.
